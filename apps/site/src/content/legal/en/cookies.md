## What we store without asking

Only your answer to the cookie notice, in your browser's local storage (`consent`). It is not a cookie, is never sent to us, and is needed to remember your choice. Our visit statistics (Plausible) use no cookies.

## What loads only if you accept

| Provider | Cookies | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram) | `_fbp`, `fr` | Measure which Meta ads bring visits and demo requests | up to 3 months |
| LinkedIn | `li_sugr`, `bcookie`, `lidc`, `UserMatchHistory`, `AnalyticsSyncHistory`, `li_fat_id` | Measure which LinkedIn ads bring visits and demo requests | up to 1 year |

## Changing your mind

*Reject* loads nothing. You can withdraw or give consent at any time from **Cookie settings** in the footer of every page; withdrawing reloads the page without these scripts.
