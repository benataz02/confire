## What we collect, and why

- **The demo request form:** your name, work email, company, SAP setup and what you write. We use them only to reply to you and arrange the demo. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (GDPR art. 6.1.b).
- **Visit statistics:** we count visits with Plausible Analytics, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (art. 6.1.f).
- **Advertising measurement, only if you accept:** the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag tell us which ads bring visitors and requests. They load only after you choose *Accept* in the cookie notice. Legal basis: your consent (art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time from *Cookie settings* in the footer. See the [cookie policy](/cookies/).
