Security

Your SAP stays on your server.

Confire never holds your SAP login and never gets a door into your network.

  • No SAP passwords in the cloudThe agent keeps them on your server. Confire never sees them.
  • An allowlist, not an open doorThe agent passes on only the request types Confire needs and refuses everything else, on your side.
  • No inbound portsThe agent dials out through an encrypted tunnel. Your firewall stays exactly as it is.

FAQ

Questions IT asks first.

Something else? Ask it in the demo, with your own setup in front of us.

Book a demo
Does Confire work with on-premise SAP Business One?

Yes, that's what it's built for. A small agent on your SAP server connects Confire to the Service Layer, with nothing to open in your firewall.

Do I need Beas?

No. With Beas, Confire also costs your routings. Without it, costs come from the BOM and your SAP price lists.

Which AI does it use, and what does it see?

Draft: Claude, by Anthropic. It sees the request you drop and the product it's filling, nothing else in SAP.

What happens if SAP is offline?

You keep configuring and pricing from Confire's copy of your options and prices. Posting to SAP waits until it's back.

Can my customers use it?

Yes, through their own portal, by invitation. They see only their own documents.

How long does setup take?

Draft: the agent installs in minutes. Modelling your first product is what the demo is for.
